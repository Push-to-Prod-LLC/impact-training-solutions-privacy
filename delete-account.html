Account & Data Deletion

Impact e-Learning (Kelly Anderson Group) — Last updated: June 3, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Impact e-Learning account and the data associated with it. Deleting your account removes your login account and personal details from the app.

How to Request Deletion

To request deletion of your account and associated data, send an email to the address below from the email address associated with your account:

  • Email: info@push-to-prod-llc.net
  • Subject line: Impact e-Learning Account Deletion Request

Please include the email address you use to sign in to the app so we can locate and remove your account. We may contact you to verify your identity before processing the request.

What Gets Deleted

When your deletion request is processed, your application account is permanently removed, including:

  • Your account and login credentials (authentication record)
  • Your email address as stored in the app account system
  • Your first and last name as stored in the app account system

If you enabled biometric login (Face ID or fingerprint), those credentials are stored only on your own device and are never sent to us. They are removed automatically when you delete the app from your device.

What Is Retained

Your training and course completion records are stored in our learning platform (SkyPrep) and are retained by Kelly Anderson Group as part of required training recordkeeping. These records may include your name and email address, because they identify whose training was completed. They are kept for compliance, regulatory, and recordkeeping purposes and are not deleted when you delete your app account.

If you have questions about the retention of your training records, contact Kelly Anderson Group directly.

Timeframe

Deletion requests are completed within 30 days of verification. You will receive a confirmation once your account and associated data have been deleted.
